Short Bio

Dr. Hemantkumar S. Goklani is currently working as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Surat. He completed his Ph.D. from Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT), Surat. He holds an M.Tech. degree from Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT), Surat, a B.E. degree from Sankalchand Patel College of Engineering (SPCE), Visnagar, affiliated to Hemchandracharya North Gujarat University (HNGU), and a Diploma in Engineering from B & B Institute of Technology (BBIT), Vallabh Vidyanagar, affiliated to the Technical Examination Board (TEB), Gandhinagar. His research interests include Digital Image Processing, Signal Processing, and the Internet of Things (IoT). He has published his research work in several reputed international journals and conferences and actively serves as a reviewer for various international peer-reviewed journals and conferences. He has also served as the first and former Head of the Department and as a former Examination Coordinator at the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Surat.
